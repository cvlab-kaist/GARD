TL;DR

GARD performs diffusion-based denoising directly in the geometry-aware feature space of a feed-forward 3D reconstructor — not in pixel space or compressed VAE latents — enabling simultaneous recovery of accurate 3D geometry and high-quality multi-view imagery from degraded inputs.

MOTIVATION

Pixel-Space and VAE-Based Restoration Fall Short for 3D Reconstruction

Feed-forward 3D reconstruction models perform well under clean conditions, but fail under real-world degradations such as motion blur.

Where should restoration happen?

⚠️ Pixel-space restoration: Ignores cross-view consistency

⚠️ Multi-view restoration in VAE-based latent space: Loses fine-grained spatial details

💡 GARD: Restores directly in the geometry-aware feature space

Key Insight: The reconstructor’s intermediate feature space preserves both cross-view consistency and visual detail, making it more suitable for restoration than pixel space or compressed VAE latents.

METHOD

Overall Framework

1

Encode Features: Degraded multi-view images are encoded by a frozen multi-view encoder to produce layer-wise latent representations.

2

Representation Denoising: The GARD denoiser restores these degraded features using a multi-view diffusion model with interleaved frame-level and global cross-view attention.

3

Task-Specific Decoding: The restored representations are decoded by their respective decoders to produce geometry predictions and restored RGB images.
